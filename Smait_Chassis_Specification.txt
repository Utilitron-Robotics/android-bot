# Smait Chassis Specification
## Intelligent Mobile Robot Platform - smBaseD

## 1. Product Overview
smBaseD, our intelligent mobile robot platform is built to serve the needs of our robots, forming the core structure for all robotic solutions in the Smait product line. Designed for durability, adaptability, and precision, this chassis is integral to the development, scalability, and deployment of Smait technologies across various applications. Featuring advanced mobility systems, modular compatibility, and robust materials, smBaseD seamlessly integrates cutting-edge technologies, including multi-sensor fusion, SLAM-based positioning and mapping, multiple operational modes, and coordinated multi-robot operation. Enhanced by cloud-based management, IoT control systems, automated charging, and flexible motion control, it ensures exceptional adaptability, precision, and efficiency while minimizing operational complexity and costs in dynamic environments.

## 3.4 Product External Connection Interface
1: Power Port: 24V/10A with overcurrent and short circuit protection, and current monitoring.
   - 1.1: Positive
   - 1.3: Negative

2: USB Expansion Interface: USB 2.0 expansion port x2.

3: Network Port: Used for chassis communication, TCP/IP protocol.
   - Upper Port: Upper Computer Camera
   - Lower Port: External USB

4: Switch Interface: Used to connect emergency stop devices.
   - 4.2 and 4.3 ports are for emergency stop
   - 4.5 and 4.6 are for system startup

PIN Pitch Connector: 5.08mm and 3.81mm
