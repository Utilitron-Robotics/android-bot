# Robotics Floor Tour Scripts

## Tour Introduction
"Welcome to the Robotics Floor at Frontier Tower! I'm your automated tour guide today. Please follow me as I show you our cutting-edge robotics research and development facilities. For your safety, please stay with the group and don't touch any equipment unless invited to do so."

## Waypoint Scripts

### Reception / Start
"Welcome to the fourth floor robotics division. This floor houses our autonomous systems laboratory, AI research center, and robotics testing facilities. We have over 50 engineers and researchers working on next-generation robotics solutions."

### Armin (Lab 1)
"This is the Armin Laboratory, named after our lead robotics engineer. Here we develop advanced manipulation systems and robotic arms. The lab features state-of-the-art motion capture systems and force-feedback testing equipment. Our team has published over 30 papers on robotic manipulation in the past year."

### Avatar (Demo Area)
"Welcome to the Avatar demonstration area. This is where we showcase our telepresence and virtual reality integration systems. Visitors can experience controlling robots remotely through our VR interfaces. The avatar system allows operators to embody robots from anywhere in the world with less than 50 millisecond latency."

### Emerson (Conference Room)
"This is the Emerson conference room, our primary meeting space for client demonstrations and team collaborations. The room is equipped with holographic projection systems and can connect to any of our robots for live demonstrations. Many important partnerships have been forged in this room."

### OpenDroids (Open Lab)
"Welcome to the OpenDroids laboratory, our open-source robotics development center. This lab focuses on creating accessible robotics solutions that can be freely shared with the global community. Over 2000 developers worldwide contribute to our open-source projects."

### Sentieverse (AI Lab)
"This is Sentieverse, our artificial intelligence and machine learning laboratory. Here we develop the cognitive systems that power our robots' decision-making capabilities. Our neural networks are trained on petabytes of real-world interaction data."

### Utilitron (Utility Robots)
"The Utilitron workshop specializes in practical service robots for everyday tasks. From delivery robots like myself to cleaning and maintenance bots, this lab focuses on making robots truly useful in daily operations. We currently have 15 different utility robot models in production."

### Empty_1 / Empty_2 (Flex Spaces)
"These flexible workspace areas can be rapidly reconfigured for different projects. They often house visiting researchers or serve as testing grounds for experimental prototypes. The modular design allows us to adapt to any research need within hours."

### Kitchen
"This is our break room and kitchen area. Even robotics engineers need coffee! Fun fact: we're testing an autonomous barista robot that can make 42 different types of coffee drinks. Feel free to grab some water if you need refreshment."

### End (Tour Completion)
"This concludes our tour of the Robotics Floor. Thank you for visiting! If you have any questions, our reception staff will be happy to help. We hope you've enjoyed learning about our work in advancing robotics technology. Have a wonderful day!"

## Motion-Triggered Greeting

"Hello! I notice you're interested in a tour. Would you like to explore our Robotics Floor? I can show you our amazing labs and demonstration areas. Just press the Start Tour button on my screen to begin!"

## Notes for Implementation

1. Each script should have a 2-3 second pause after delivery to allow for processing
2. Arrival announcements should play the "arrival" sound before speaking
3. Keep walking speed slow during narration (0.3 m/s)
4. Stop completely when delivering longer explanations
5. Display relevant images or lab logos on tablet during each stop
6. Wait times at each stop: 10-15 seconds for short tours, 20-30 seconds for full tours
